# Problem Statement

## What we solve

1. Fast to enter: photo, size, cost and price are all a person can do on a phone while standing in the shop.
2. Easy to find a pair: search by ID, or filter by size and category when a customer asks for “size 42 sneakers”.
3. No integration work: until the business is registered, the M-PESA code is the link between the system and the till statement.
4. Still valuable to the owner: the reports below are things a notebook can’t give them.

## Reports to Generate

1. Daily sales: what sold, for how much, cash versus M-PESA, and whether the total matches their till and cash.
2. Profit per pair, and by category, brand and channel: which pairs make money, and is TikTok live or the shop better.
3. Stock on hand: how many pairs are unsold and how much money is tied up in them at cost.
4. Aging stock: pairs that have sat unsold for 30, 60 or 90 days. This helps them decide when to mark down, which matters a lot in thrift.
